## Sker Ritual（Epic，免费领至 10 月 29 日）

![Sker Ritual](https://wardea.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/image-89-1024x576.png)

## Home：A Unique Horror Adventure（Epic，免费领至 10 月 29 日）

![Home：A Unique Horror Adventure](https://assets.nintendo.com/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1920/ncom/en_US/games/switch/h/home-postmortem-edition-switch/screenshot-gallery/screenshot01)

## 911 Operator（Steam，限时免费领，报道称 10 月 29 日截止）

![911 Operator](https://static1.dualshockersimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/horizontal_header.png)
